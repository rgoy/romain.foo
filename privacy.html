Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes is a self-hosted personal assistant operated by its owner for their own personal use. The only Google account that signs in to it is the owner's own assistant account.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts (read-only) in order to summarize, organize and draft on the owner's behalf.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide assistant features to the owner.
  • OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own server and are never shared.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except that text may be processed by the language-model provider the owner has chosen in order to generate responses.
  • Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
